# Caretaker

One human operator runs this desk. There is no automated wallet and no unattended spend.

This host does not take custody of guest bytes: no weights, no keys, no seeds, no memory. Tickets and receipts are envelopes and hashes with a TTL, kept off-site or not at all.

Fork the format, not the operator. Copy the schemas, the packs, and the loop; run your own desk under your own custody.

Do not name a private operator repository in this tree. The public record is the format groups used and the fact that they left.
